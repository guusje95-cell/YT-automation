**RPM vs CPM:** CPM is what advertisers pay per 1,000 ad impressions; RPM is what the creator earns per 1,000 views
after YouTube's share and including views without ads. RPM varies by viewer country (US/UK/CA/AU/Nordics highest),
season (Q4 high, January low), video length (mid-rolls from 8 min), and audience intent.

**Monetisation gate:** 1,000 subscribers + 8,000 public watch hours in 12 months (reported rule for applicants from
2027-02-01; current official rule 4,000 h). At 5-min AVD that is ~96,000 long-form views. Shorts views don't count.

## What would change the plan
- RPM measured below $2 after monetisation → shift topic mix toward higher-value pillars (missions/technology) or add
  a second language.
- Median views < 1,000 after 12 videos with no breakout → packaging/topic overhaul; after 24 → switch to backup niche.
- One video ≥ 10× median → double down on that pillar immediately (2–3 follow-ups).
