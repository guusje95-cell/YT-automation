# Car channel: format research (Pagani, cartoon/stickman, real images vs animation, character) — 2026-10-27

Method (Fact): two of our own scans on public YouTube search pages, uploads from the past year, long-form ≥ 4 min.
1. `topic_scan` on 12 format/topic candidates (`config/topic_candidates_cars_formats.yaml`,
   report `research/topic_scan_2026-10-27_cars_formats.md`).
2. New `format_study`: 16 viewer-style car-story queries; every video ≥ 100k views (48), channel size looked up, thumbnails
   put on contact sheets (`research/raw/format_study_2026-10-27_cars_*.jpg`) and classified by hand by visual format.
Public counts at scan time; a decision aid, not a forecast.

## 1. Pagani
| Query group | Top-8 median views | Small-channel breakouts |
|---|---|---|
| Pagani story (Horacio Pagani) | 798 | 0 |
| Pagani Utopia / Zonda engineering | 74k | 0 |
| Pagani vs Koenigsegg vs Bugatti | 1.09M | 1 (Tiny Track Cars, 423k) |

Fact: Pagani videos that win are almost all big presenter channels with the real car (carwow "Why Pagani BOLTS cost more
than a Porsche 911!" 1.37M; Top Gear 1.10M; Chris Slix, abandoned Pagani engine, 1.35M). The founder story as a search
topic is close to empty, and Pagani's own five-part history series already exists on its official channel.
Judgment: Pagani is a strong *thumbnail name*, not a strong *standalone topic* for us. Use it inside a bigger hook
(rivalry, obsession, "the most expensive bolt", hypercar records) instead of a biography.

## 2. Stickman / cartoon
Fact: the stickman query group scored high (4.6M median) but every top result is something else: stickman ragdoll
"dismount" compilations, Pixar *Cars* for kids, zombie survival cartoons, children's English-learning cat stories.
In the 48 car-story winners there is **no** stickman or 2D-cartoon video. "Animated car history (2D)" has 0 breakouts.
Judgment: no evidence that cartoon/stickman works for car stories; it would also mean building a whole animation
pipeline. Not for now; at most a later Shorts experiment.

## 3. What winning car-story videos actually look like (48 videos ≥ 100k, past year)
Small channels (< 100k subs) that broke out, by visual format (Fact, hand-classified from thumbnails/titles):
| Format | Examples (views, subs) |
|---|---|
| Real photos + motion graphics + narration (faceless documentary) | CoreSight Jaguar 2.54M (24k); JunkBondInvestor Jaguar 670k (78k); Motor Zen BYD 646k (31k); Engine Era Saab 631k (2.3k); Vintage Wheels worst cars 686k (9k); Bakaish Verstappen 734k / 866k (9k); The Sleeper List Jaguar 756k (43k) |
| 3D-rendered technical animation | Motoring Masters quattro vs xDrive vs 4MATIC 2.76M (99.7k) |
| Tier list / graphics + photos | JIMMYDRIVES 1.04M (55k); Car Clever 1.72M (12k, stock presenter) |
| Real race footage | ZERO MARGIN Isle of Man TT 899k (7k) — footage rights risk |
Big channels: mostly presenters with the real car (carwow, Engineering Explained, Top Gear, MKBHD); the one big
faceless exception is fern (3D animation, 5.8M).
Read: for a faceless newcomer, the proven path is **true stories with drama (downfall, scandal, rivalry, record) told
with real photos, strong motion graphics and data**, with 3D animation as the premium version. That is the format of
our pilot.

## 4. Fictional adventurous character?
Fact: none of the 48 winners uses an invented character. The protagonists are real: a company (Jaguar, Saab, BYD), a
driver (Verstappen), a founder. Car viewers come for real cars and real people.
Judgment: no fictional adventurer on the car channel (that worked as a vehicle for space because nobody can film
inside Jupiter). Instead: always follow a real protagonist with stakes (Jenatzy vs Chasseloup, SSC vs its critics,
BYD vs the 500 wall) and keep the narrator as the channel's recurring voice.

## 5. Policy check
YouTube's "inauthentic content" monetisation update (announced July 2025): targets mass-produced, templated,
repetitive videos; faceless/AI-assisted channels stay eligible when there is original research, scripting and custom
visuals (Gulf News, PlagiarismToday, Creator Liaison statements). Supports: original research + own graphics per
episode, not a fixed template with swapped stock clips.

## Decision proposal
- Keep the pilot format: true story, real licence-clean photos, own motion graphics (record charts, speed counters,
  road/POV shots, timing illustrations), data, no fictional character.
- Next upgrades: more original visual variety per episode (a 3D render step is the premium option), Pagani as part of
  a rivalry/obsession episode, Jaguar/BYD/Saab-style downfall stories as the next topics.
- Validate with our own retention data once the channel is live (first-minute drop, average view %).
