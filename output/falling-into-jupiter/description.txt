On 7 December 1995 a small NASA probe plunged into Jupiter at 170,000 km/h and sent data for 58 minutes before it died. We follow it down through the clouds, then keep going with NASA's Juno data, past an ocean of liquid metal to a core nobody expected. Real mission data, every number sourced below.
