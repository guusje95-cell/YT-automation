Based on a true story: in 1995 NASA's Galileo probe fell into Jupiter and sent data for 58 minutes. Mara Voss and her capsule are a dramatization; every fact about Jupiter in this film is real. We follow her capsule from the top of Jupiter's atmosphere past the exact depth where NASA's Galileo probe went silent in 1995, then down through an ocean of liquid metal to a core nobody expected, using real data from Galileo and Juno.
