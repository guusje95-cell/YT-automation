Based on a true story: in 1957 two astronomers in Mexico catalogued a faint blue dot as number 618. It turned out to be one of the brightest objects in the universe, powered by the heaviest black hole ever weighed. Jonah and Iris are a dramatization; every fact about TON 618 and black holes in this film is real and sourced below.
