A true story. In October 2020 a small American company claimed its car had averaged more than 500 km/h on a Nevada highway. Within weeks its own video had been pulled apart. This is the 127-year fight over one title, the fastest car in the world, and the two rules that decide who really owns it. This November a Chinese electric hypercar will try to break 500 km/h.
